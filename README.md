# ringside-site

Marketing URL: https://kobrinsam.github.io/ringside-site/
Support URL: https://kobrinsam.github.io/ringside-site/support.html
Privacy policy URL: https://kobrinsam.github.io/ringside-site/privacy.html

Public website for the Ringside: Horse Show Scores iOS app.

- `privacy.html` is the privacy policy (App Store Connect privacy policy URL).
- `index.html` is the marketing page (App Store Connect Marketing URL).
- `support.html` is the support page (App Store Connect Support URL).
- `assets/` holds app screenshots and the horse logo; `site.css` is shared styling; `site.js` drives the motion (all of it switches off for visitors who prefer reduced motion).

Publish with GitHub Pages (Settings > Pages > Deploy from branch `main`, folder `/ (root)`). The policy is then at https://kobrinsam.github.io/ringside-site/privacy.html.
