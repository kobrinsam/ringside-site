Common questions

How do I join a show?

On the home screen tap Join with a code and enter the code the organizer gave you. Public shows also appear under Find a show.

I was invited as a judge but can't see the class

Sign in with the same email address the organizer invited, and verify that email. Then open the invitation on the home screen and accept it. If it still doesn't show, ask the organizer to check the email they used.

How do I enter a class?

Open the show, go to Classes, choose a class and tap to enter. Add your horse's name and, if the show asks for it, a horse ID. The office approves your entry and assigns a back number.

When do I see results?

Results appear after the show office reviews and publishes them. Until then the class shows as unpublished.

A judge signed a sheet by mistake

A signed sheet is locked so results can't be changed quietly. Contact the show organizer, who can contact us if a correction is needed.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen enter your email and tap Forgot password. We'll email you a reset link.

Which tests are included?

The library covers dressage (FEI, USDF/USEF, Western Dressage), eventing, working equitation, reining and more, including the 2027 USDF/USEF tests effective 1 December 2026. Missing a test? Tell us.

Delete your account

Email outfits.oracle.app@gmail.com from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my Ringside account". We will delete your account and personal data within 30 days and confirm by email. See the privacy policy for what is kept.
