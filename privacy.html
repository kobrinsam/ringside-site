Ringside Privacy Policy

App: Ringside: Horse Show Scores (iOS) · Effective date: 8 November 2026

Ringside helps horse show organizers, judges, riders and spectators run shows and see scores. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who can see it, and the choices you have. We have tried to keep it short and plain.

1. Who we are

Ringside is published by its developer ("we", "us"). Contact: outfits.oracle.app@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

We only collect what the app needs to work. You give us this information yourself, by creating an account or by using features in the app.

InformationWhy we need it
Email address and password (your password is handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see or store it in readable form)To create your account and sign you in.
Name (the display name you choose)To show who you are to a show's organizers, judges and other participants, and on start lists, score sheets and results.
Horse names and optional horse or passport ID numbers, and the optional details you add to your profile: a mobile phone number and a federation licence or judge numberTo enter you and your horse in classes and print them on start lists, score sheets and results.
Show activity: shows you create or join, entries and back numbers, invitations you send or accept, your role in a show (for example owner, office, judge or rider)To run the show and decide what each person is allowed to see and do.
Scores, comments and judges' signatures (including the signing judge's name and the time of signing)To produce results and keep an accurate record of each ride.
Email addresses you type for other people, for example when an organizer invites a judge or adds an entry for a rider who has no account yetTo match the invitation or entry to that person when they sign in. See section 5.

What we do not collect. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, health or financial information, or advertising identifiers. The app has no payments, no advertising and no analytics or crash-reporting tools.

3. How we use information

We use your information only to provide the app: to sign you in, to run shows, to enter riders, to record and publish scores, and to keep the service secure. We do not sell your information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not use it to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

4. Who can see your information

  • Other people in a show. Your name, horse, back number, entry status and published results can be seen by other signed-in people who join or browse that show. Published results (placings and scores) are visible to signed-in people who can view that show. Full score sheets, including judges' comments, are visible only to the judge who wrote them and the show's office.
  • Your account details (such as your email address) are visible to you and, where needed to run a show you have joined, to its organizers.
  • Service provider. The app is built on Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), provided by Google LLC. Google stores and processes the data described above on our behalf. Google's own handling of data is described in the Firebase privacy information.
  • Legal reasons. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

We do not share your information with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Email addresses you enter for someone else

If you are a show organizer, you may type another person's email address to invite them or to add an entry for them. Only enter an address when you have a good reason to contact that person for the show. The invitation is sent by your own mail or messages app, not by our servers. An address you enter for an entry is kept in a private record that only show staff can read, and it is used only to attach the entry to that person's account when they sign in.

6. How long we keep information

We keep your account information while your account is active. Show records, entries and signed scores are kept as long as the show exists, because results are an official record that other people rely on. When you delete your account (section 7), your sign-in, profile (name, phone, licence number and saved horses) and show list are deleted. Entries, signed scores and published results are official records of the show, so they stay with that show and may still show your name and your horse's name. You can ask us to remove or anonymize those too by emailing us, and we will do so within 30 days unless the law requires us to keep them. A show you created stays available to its other staff.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction. You can see and edit your name and profile in the app.
  • Deleting your account. In the app, open your profile (the person icon on the home screen, then Edit profile), tap Delete account and enter your password to confirm. Your sign-in, profile, saved horses and show list are deleted immediately and can't be recovered. You can also email outfits.oracle.app@gmail.com from your account's address and we will do it for you.
  • Other requests. You may ask us for a copy of your information, to correct it, or to stop using it. Depending on where you live (for example California, or the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), you may have additional legal rights over your personal data. We will honour them. Write to the address above.
  • Marketing. We do not send marketing email.

8. Children

Ringside is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Show organizers must not create accounts or enter personal information for a child under 13 without a parent or guardian's permission. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored in Google's cloud. Access is controlled by account sign-in and by security rules that limit each person to the information their role in a show allows. No online service can be perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. International use

Our service provider may process and store your information in the United States and other countries where it operates. By using the app you understand that your information may be transferred to those countries.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above, and for material changes we will tell you in the app or by email before they take effect.

12. Contact

Questions or requests: outfits.oracle.app@gmail.com.
